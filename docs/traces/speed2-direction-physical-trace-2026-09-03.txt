=====================================

monitor. The monitor sent no commands. Timestamps are seconds from monitor
start and only scan-state changes plus physical key/direction events are
listed.

The capture included setup activity before the two useful direction actions.

The repeated `0x01`/`0x00` sequence is the exhaust marker. The marker starts
about 6.6 seconds after the long press is first detected and is interleaved
with a return to the normal `0x03` speed display.

Interpretation
--------------

- Normal speed level 2 display: `0x03`.
- Intake marker: repeated `0x08` and `0x00` phases.
- Exhaust marker: repeated `0x01` and `0x00` phases.
- The controller must not infer direction from a single `0x00 -> 0x01` wake
  transition; repeated marker cycles are required.
- The controller-generated direction hold needs to remain active long enough
  for the delayed marker sequence to begin.
